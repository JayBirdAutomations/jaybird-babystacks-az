01 — Our story

From red velvet stacks to loco moco

BabyStacks Cafe has been serving breakfast since 2010, with signature pancakes, Filipino-Hawaiian specialties and classic breakfast favorites made fresh and served daily.

The menu runs from lemon ricotta and banana cream pie pancakes to ube crepes, kalua pig benedicts, sisig and Portuguese fried rice.

You'll find us across Las Vegas, with a Henderson location opening soon.
